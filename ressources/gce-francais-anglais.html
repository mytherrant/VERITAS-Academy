GCE O Level & A Level (Cameroon) : English, French & subjects

The GCE Board exams

In the English subsystem, students sit the GCE Ordinary Level (Form 5) and Advanced Level (Upper Sixth). Papers combine MCQ (Paper 1) and essay/structured questions (Paper 2).

Grading you should know

O Level: grades A, B, C = pass (U = fail). A Level: grades A–E = pass (F = fail). Core subjects include English Language, Literature in English and French.
