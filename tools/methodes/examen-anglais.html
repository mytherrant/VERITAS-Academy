La compréhension : répondre « in your own words »

  1. Read the questions first, then the passage.
  2. Underline the part of the text that answers each question.
  3. Answer in a full sentence, with your own words; do not copy whole lines.
  4. For opinion questions, give a clear yes or no and one reason.
Exemple (question du BEPC 2021)« Do you think it is necessary to be obedient and generous? Justify your answer. » Réponse : « Yes, I think it is necessary, because obedient and generous people are trusted and helped by others. »
Exemple (Probatoire 2025)Question : « How did they know that he had been killed by a wild animal? » Réponse : « They knew it because they found his canoe stained with blood. »

Pour une lettre, la mise en page est notée à part : adresse de l'expéditeur, date, formule d'appel (« Dear Sir, »), formule finale (« Yours faithfully, »). Pour un discours : titre, salutation de l'auditoire, formule de clôture.

Sujet d'article (BAC A 2025)You are the Senior Prefect of your school and you have realised that violence is on the rise. Write an article for the school newspaper titled « Violence, the cankerworm in our schools today ». Plan modèle : a title; an introduction that states the problem; one paragraph on the causes; one paragraph on the consequences; one paragraph of solutions; a short conclusion and your name and position.

L'essai

Un paragraphe d'introduction, deux ou trois paragraphes qui développent chacun une idée, une conclusion. Utilise des connecteurs simples : first, then, moreover, however, finally.

Les erreurs fréquentes

  • Choosing two topics for the composition.
  • Copying sentences from the passage in the comprehension.
  • Question tags : « You cannot travel without a passport, can you? »
  • Forgetting the « s » of the third person: « She goes to school. »

Check-list du jour J

  • All four sections answered.
  • One composition topic only, with the right format.
  • Word count checked.
  • Ten minutes to check tenses, plurals and spelling.
