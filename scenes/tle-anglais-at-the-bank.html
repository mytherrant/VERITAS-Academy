At the bank

Anglais · Terminale

Dialogue fictif écrit pour la leçon. Les faits et les dates cités sont exacts.

Objectif

Exchange information about banking services (opening an account, depositing, withdrawing, saving, mobile money); use “for, since, ago” and link ideas with “because, so, although”.

Programme : Progression harmonisée Tle · Economic life and occupations, lessons 16-18 (using banking services), lesson 14 (adverbs: for, since, ago), lesson 17 (complex sentences)

Lieu : A bank in Bamenda · Saturday morning

Personnages

  • Mr Fon, bank clerk
  • Mary, a young tailor
  • Tanyi, her brother

La scène

Mary opened her tailoring workshop two years ago. She has been keeping her money in a box under her bed since then. Today she wants to open a savings account. — Mary a ouvert son atelier de couture il y a deux ans. Depuis, elle garde son argent dans une boîte sous son lit. Aujourd’hui, elle veut ouvrir un compte d’épargne.

for, since, ago

“ago” follows a duration and goes with the simple past: two years ago (il y a deux ans). “for” + a duration, “since” + a starting point, usually with the present perfect: for two years, since 2024.

Tanyi, her brother : Why go to the bank? Your box is safe, isn’t it? — Pourquoi aller à la banque ? Ta boîte est sûre, non ?

Mary, a young tailor : It isn’t safe, because thieves or fire could take everything. Although the bank charges fees, my money will be protected. — Elle n’est pas sûre, parce que des voleurs ou un incendie pourraient tout emporter. Même si la banque prend des frais, mon argent sera protégé.

At the counter.

Mr Fon, bank clerk : Good morning, madam. How can I help you? — Bonjour, madame. Que puis-je pour vous ?

Mary, a young tailor : I would like to open a savings account. What documents do I need? — Je voudrais ouvrir un compte d’épargne. De quels documents ai-je besoin ?

Mr Fon, bank clerk : Your national identity card, two passport photographs, and a location plan of your home. The minimum first deposit is twenty-five thousand francs. — Votre carte nationale d’identité, deux photos d’identité et un plan de localisation de votre domicile. Le premier dépôt minimum est de vingt-cinq mille francs.

Workshop · make a deposit

Mary deposits twenty-five thousand francs. Count out exactly 25,000 FCFA.

Deposit: 25,000 FCFA. “I would like to deposit twenty-five thousand francs.”

Workshop · banking words

Match each English word with its meaning.

  • Put money in : to deposit ; to save
  • Take money out : to withdraw ; a cash withdrawal at an ATM
  • Money you borrow : a loan

deposit = dépôt, withdraw = retirer, loan = prêt, ATM = distributeur.

Mr Fon, bank clerk : You can also link your account to your mobile money wallet, so you can send money from your phone. — Vous pouvez aussi relier votre compte à votre portefeuille de mobile money, pour envoyer de l’argent depuis votre téléphone.

À toi de décider : You are Mary. The clerk offers you a loan to buy a second sewing machine. What do you do?

  • I accept immediately, without asking about the interest rate. — Risky: you may pay back much more than you borrowed.
  • I ask about the interest rate and the monthly repayments, then I compare with my income. — A responsible decision: you borrow only what you can repay.
  • I refuse all loans forever. — Safe, but you may miss a chance to grow your business.

Question : What does “interest rate” mean?

  • Le taux d’intérêt : the percentage you pay the bank for borrowing. (bonne réponse)
  • Mon intérêt personnel pour la banque.

Or the percentage the bank pays you on your savings.

Tanyi, her brother : So I could open an account too, couldn’t I? — Alors je pourrais ouvrir un compte moi aussi, non ?

Mr Fon, bank clerk : Yes, we have a youth savings account for students. — Oui, nous avons un compte d’épargne jeunes pour les élèves et étudiants.

Quiz

  1. “I opened my shop two years …” — ago ago + simple past.
  2. What does “to withdraw money” mean? — Retirer de l’argent The opposite of “to deposit”.
  3. Which document is needed to open an account? — A national identity card. With photos and a location plan.
  4. Complete: “… the bank charges fees, my money is safe.” — Although Although = bien que, même si.
  5. What should you ask before taking a loan? — The interest rate and the monthly repayments. Then compare with your income.

À retenir

  • Banking vocabulary: account, savings account, deposit, withdraw, loan, interest rate, ATM, fees, mobile money.
  • ago + simple past (two years ago); for + duration; since + starting point.
  • Linking words: because (cause), so (consequence), although (concession).
  • Before a loan: interest rate, monthly repayments, duration.

À écrire

Write a short paragraph (eight to ten lines) to convince a friend to open a savings account. Use “because”, “so” and “although”.
