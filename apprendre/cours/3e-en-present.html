Revise the present tenses: simple and continuous

Pour commencer

« I am knowing you since one year » : Kwame, your pen pal in Accra, does not understand your message.

A WhatsApp chat with a Ghanaian pen pal is your guiding thread: in each part, a clue helps you choose between three ideas.

À la fin de la leçon, tu sauras :
  • use the simple present for habits, routines and general truths
  • use the present continuous for actions happening now
  • recognise time markers (every day, now, at the moment…)
  • avoid the continuous with state verbs (know, like, want…)
Situation-problème

Junior's message to Kwame

Kwame asks: « What do you do every Saturday? » Junior answers: « Every Saturday I am going to the market with Mama. Now I write to you. I am knowing you since one year! » Kwame replies: « Are you going to the market right now? I don't understand. »

Junior's sister says: « The -ing form is more modern, use it everywhere! » His cousin says: « In English, both tenses mean the same thing. »

Document 1 · Time markers
Simple presentevery day, every Saturday, always, usually, often, sometimes, never
Present continuousnow, right now, at the moment, today, look!, listen!

Which tense should Junior use in each sentence, and why?

Ta tâche : Correct Junior's three sentences and explain each correction with a rule and a time marker.

Les hypothèses à trancher

  1. The -ing form is more modern: use it everywhere.
  2. Both tenses mean the same thing.
  3. The simple present is for habits; the continuous is for actions happening now; state verbs stay in the simple present.

The simple present

For habits, routines and general truths.

I / you / we / they play — he / she / it plays

  • Negative: She does not (doesn't) play.
  • Question: Does she play?
  • Markers: every day, always, usually, often, sometimes, never.
Don't forget the -sWith he / she / it: she goes, he watches, Mama buys. Questions and negatives use do / does: Does she sell plantains? She doesn't sell rice.

Subject + verb (-s with he / she / it) · do / does + not + verb · Do / Does + subject + verb?

ExemplesEvery Saturday, I go to the market with my mother.
My father works in a bank. Does he work on Sundays? No, he doesn't.
SpellingSpelling of -s: watch → watches, go → goes, study → studies, play → plays.
Planche 3D : The alarm clock of habitsEvery day, the alarm clock rings at 6 o'clock: habits and routines are expressed with the simple present. « Every Saturday I go to the market. » La manipuler dans Apprendre.
Indice 1

« Every Saturday » is a habit marker: Junior must write « Every Saturday I go to the market ». With « I am going », Kwame thought he was going right now.

  • Hypothèse 1 rejetée : « Every Saturday » is a habit: the simple present is required.
À retenirHabits, routines, general truths → simple present (+ -s with he / she / it).

The present continuous

For actions happening now or temporary situations.

am / is / are + verb-ing

  • I am reading. They are playing.
  • Markers: now, right now, at the moment, look!, listen!

Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing

Present continuousAn action in progress now, or a temporary situation.
ExemplesLook! The bus is coming.
My father usually works in Douala, but this month he is working in Garoua.
TipMarkers decide: every day, usually, often → simple present; now, at the moment, look!, listen! → present continuous.

Simulation interactive : Time markers: choose the right tense (dans Apprendre)

Indice 2

« Now » marks an action in progress: « Now I am writing to you ». The two tenses do not mean the same thing: one describes habits, the other describes what is happening at the moment of speaking.

  • Hypothèse 2 rejetée : « I go every Saturday » (habit) ≠ « I am going now » (in progress).
À retenirNow, at the moment, look!, listen! → am / is / are + verb-ing.

Verbs that don't like -ing

State verbs are usually not used in the continuous: know, like, love, want, need, understand, believe.

Wrong / RightI am knowing → I know. She is wanting rice → She wants rice.
Since one year?« I am knowing you since one year » has two mistakes: know is a state verb, and English uses the present perfect with for: « I have known you for one year. » At your level, you can also say: « I know you very well. »
Indice 3

Know is a state verb: never « am knowing ». The sister's rule (« -ing everywhere ») creates mistakes.

  • Hypothèse 3 validée : Habit → simple present; now → continuous; state verbs → simple form.
À retenirknow, like, love, want, need, understand, believe: no -ing.

Situation résolue : Junior's corrected message

  1. « Every Saturday » → habit → simple present: I go (clue 1).
  2. « Now » → action in progress → I am writing (clue 2).
  3. « know » is a state verb → I know / I have known you for one year (clue 3).

Corrected message: « Every Saturday I go to the market with Mama. Right now I am writing to you. I have known you for one year! » The simple present expresses habits and routines, with markers like every Saturday; the present continuous expresses an action happening at the moment of speaking, with markers like now; state verbs such as know, like or want are not used in the continuous. The two tenses do not mean the same thing, and the -ing form is not « more modern »: it is simply for actions in progress.

À toi de transférer

Write a WhatsApp message of five lines to Kwame: describe your usual Sunday (simple present) and what your family is doing right now (present continuous).

Fiche de révision

  • Simple present: habits, facts; -s with he/she/it.
  • do/does for negatives and questions.
  • Present continuous: am/is/are + -ing, for now.
  • State verbs (know, like, want…) stay simple.
  • Habits: every day, always, usually → simple present.
  • Now, at the moment, look!, listen! → present continuous.

Exercice

Put the verbs in the correct tense: 1. Look! The baby (sleep). 2. My father (work) in Garoua. 3. I (not understand) this word. (is sleeping ; works ; don't understand)

Quiz

  1. She ___ to church every Sunday.
    Réponse : goes — Habit: simple present with -s.
  2. Listen! Somebody ___ .
    Réponse : is singing — « Listen! » = now.
  3. I ___ the answer.
    Réponse : know — State verb: no -ing.
  4. Every Saturday I ___ to the market.
    Réponse : go — Habit marker: every Saturday.
  5. Look! The baby ___ .
    Réponse : is sleeping — « Look! » = now.
  6. My father ___ in Garoua.
    Réponse : works — A general fact.
  7. Does she ___ plantains?
    Réponse : sell — After does: base form.
  8. Which marker goes with the present continuous?
    Réponse : at the moment — Action in progress.
  9. Which verb is a state verb?
    Réponse : want — want, like, know…
  10. Now I ___ to you.
    Réponse : am writing — « Now »: in progress.
